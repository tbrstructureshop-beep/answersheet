Writing Task 1 Question Prompt:
You should spend about 20 minutes on this task.
The line graph below shows the percentage of households with internet access in three countries (Country A, Country B, and Country C) between 2000 and 2020.
Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant. Write at least 150 words.
WHAT EXAMINERS LOOK FOR UNDER GRAMMATICAL RANGE & ACCURACY (GRA)
Assessment Foundation
1. Range of Complex Structures:
Use of non-defining relative clauses, participial adverbials (reaching, climbing, culminating), passive voice, and comparative subordination.
2. Proportion of Error-Free Sentences:
Band 7 requires "frequent error-free sentences"; Band 8 demands "the majority of sentences are error-free".
3. Tense & Aspect Consistency:
Accurate use of past simple for historical timelines (2000-2020), past perfect where sequence matters, and present simple for graphic descriptions.
4. Punctuation Control:
Proper deployment of commas in complex compound sentences, parenthetical dashes, and semicolons to link closely related comparative clauses.
