LEXICAL RESOURCE
IELTS TASK 1
LEXICAL BENCHMARK: VOCABULARY, STYLE & COLLOCATION
Band Descriptor Focus
Writing Task 1 Question Prompt:
You should spend about 20 minutes on this task.
The line graph below shows the percentage of households with internet access in three countries (Country A, Country B, and Country C) between 2000 and 2020.
Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant. Write at least 150 words.
KEY EVALUATION DOMAINS FOR LEXICAL RESOURCE (LR)
Assessment Foundation
1. Range & Flexibility:
Ability to avoid repetitive verbs (went up, was) by utilizing precise trend terminology (accelerated, surged, crested, climbed steadily).
2. Precision & Collocation:
Natural pairings of academic words (e.g., penetration rate, narrowing the disparity, unbroken expansion, baseline figure).
3. Awareness of Academic Style:
Avoiding informal or conversational terms (a lot of money, poor, catch up) in favor of formal objective phrases.
4. Spelling & Word Formation:
Accurate noun-verb-adjective transformations (expand → expansion; accelerate → acceleration; three → threefold).
