COHERENCE & COHESION
IELTS TASK 1
CORE DISCOURSE STIMULUS: COHERENCE & COHESION CRITERIA
Band Descriptor Focus
Writing Task 1 Question Prompt:
You should spend about 20 minutes on this task.
The line graph below shows the percentage of households with internet access in three countries (Country A, Country B, and Country C) between 2000 and 2020.
Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant. Write at least 150 words.
WHAT EXAMINERS LOOK FOR UNDER COHERENCE & COHESION (CC)
Assessment Foundation
1. Logical Progression (Macro-Coherence):
Information must advance logically from introduction to overview, followed by grouping of data without disjointed jumping between categories.
2. Cohesive Devices (Micro-Cohesion):
Connectors, transition signals, and conjunctions must be accurate, varied, and not repetitive or mechanically placed at every sentence start.
3. Referencing & Substitution:
Skillful use of pronouns and noun phrases (this metric, the former, that of, the market leader) to avoid repeating exact country names or numbers.
4. Logical Paragraphing:
Clear thematic separation between the global overview and discrete body paragraphs (e.g., contrasting the top tier vs. the laggard).
