Task 1 Benchmark: Task Achievement (Band 1 - Band 9)
REFERENCE QUESTION
IELTS TASK 1
OFFICIAL STIMULUS: TASK 1 ACADEMIC GRAPH
Standard Dataset
Writing Task 1 Question Prompt:
You should spend about 20 minutes on this task.
The line graph below shows the percentage of households with internet access in three countries (Country A, Country B, and Country C) between 2000 and 2020.
Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant. Write at least 150 words.
KEY STATISTICAL REALITIES OF THIS GRAPH (FOR EXAMINER BENCHMARKING)
Assessment Core
Country A (Blue Square):
Starts highest at 40% (2000), rises in an unbroken near-linear trajectory through 70% (2010), peaking at 85.5% (2020). Consistently leads throughout.
Country B (Red Circle):
Starts at 15.5% (2000), demonstrates the sharpest surge (especially post-2005), jumping from 48% (2010) to 75.5% (2020), nearly closing the gap with Country A.
Country C (Green Triangle):
Starts lowest at 5% (2000), climbs steadily to 20% (2010) and finishes at 35.5% (2020). Despite a sevenfold rise, it stays consistently lowest.
Crucial Global Overview Requirement:
Must observe: (1) All 3 countries grew, (2) Country A always highest vs Country C always lowest, (3) Country B saw the fastest acceleration.
