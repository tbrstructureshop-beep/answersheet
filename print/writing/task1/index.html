Skilfully manages paragraphing.Uses a wide range of vocabulary with very natural and sophisticated control of lexical features.Uses a wide range of structures with full flexibility and accuracy; rare minor errors.
8Covers all requirements sufficiently. Highlights and clearly illustrates key features/bullet points.Sequences information logically. Manages all aspects of cohesion well. Uses paragraphing sufficiently.Uses a wide vocabulary fluently and flexibly to convey precise meanings. Occasional inaccuracies.Uses a wide range of structures. The majority of sentences are error-free.
7Covers the requirements. Presents a clear overview of main trends. Highlights key features but could be more fully extended.Logically organises information; clear progression throughout. Uses a range of cohesive devices appropriately.Uses sufficient vocabulary to allow flexibility. Uses less common lexical items with some awareness of style.Uses a variety of complex structures. Produces frequent error-free sentences. Good control of grammar.
6Addresses the requirements. Presents an overview with information appropriately selected. Details may be irrelevant/inaccurate.Arranges information coherently. Uses cohesive devices effectively, but cohesion within/between sentences may be faulty.Uses an adequate range of vocabulary. Attempts to use less common vocabulary but with some inaccuracy.Uses a mix of simple and complex sentence forms. Makes some errors in grammar and punctuation.
5Generally addresses the task. Recounts detail mechanically with no clear overview. Data presentation may be incomplete.Presents information with some organisation but lack of progression. Inadequate or over-use of cohesive devices.Uses limited vocabulary, minimally adequate for the task. May make noticeable errors in spelling/word formation.Uses only a limited range of structures. Attempts complex sentences but these tend to be less accurate.
4Attempts to address the task but does not cover all key features. The format may be inappropriate.Presents information but it is not arranged coherently. No clear progression. Inaccurate use of cohesive devices.Uses only basic vocabulary which may be used repetitively. Errors in spelling/word formation may cause strain for the reader.Uses a very limited range of structures with only rare use of subordinate clauses. Frequent grammatical errors.
3Fails to address the task adequately. Misses key features/trends. Much detail may be irrelevant.Does not organise ideas logically. Uses a very limited range of cohesive devices.Uses only a very limited range of words and expressions with very limited control of word formation.Attempts sentence forms but errors in grammar and punctuation predominate.
2Answer is barely related to the task.Has very little control of organisational features.Uses extremely limited vocabulary; essentially isolated words.Cannot use sentence forms except in memorised phrases.
1Answer is completely unrelated to the task.Fails to communicate any message.Can only use a few isolated words.Cannot use sentence forms at all.
